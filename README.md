## About the Project 
This study investigates heterogeneity in the returns to education within Vietnam's service sector. Specifically, it aims to evaluate and compare the wage premium associated with educational attainment between high-end (knowledge-intensive) and low-end (traditional or labor-intensive) service segments.

### Build With
* **[R](https://www.r-project.org/)**: For data extraction, cleaning, feature engineering, OLS regression modeling, and data visualization (`tidyverse`, `haven`, `car`, `lmtest`, `sandwich`, `stargazer`, `ggplot2`).
* **[LaTeX](https://www.latex-project.org/)**: For drafting the academic report and generating presentation slides (Beamer).

## Data & Methodology
### Data Source
- **2018 Vietnam Labor Force Survey (LFS):** Extracted key microdata variables, resulting in a final analytical sample of $121,430$ observations.

### Empirical Model
The core empirical strategy employs an **extended Mincerian wage equation** estimated via **Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)** with robust standard errors (HC1):

$$\ln(\text{Wage}_i) = \beta_0 + \beta_1 \text{educ}_i + \beta_2 \text{servseg}_i + \beta_3 (\text{educ}_i \times \text{servseg}_i) + \gamma X_i + \epsilon_i$$

### Subsample Analysis
To capture the changing nature of the labor market and generational shifts, the dataset is stratified into two age groups:
- **Young Cohort (Aged 15 - 35):** Examines the value of formal educational credentials in the modern gig economy and digital transformation era.
- **Middle-Aged Cohort (Aged 36 - 55):** Investigates the career life-cycle and the "saturation effect" of education among seasoned workers.
